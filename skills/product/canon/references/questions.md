> canon-version: 2026-08
> The question bank, tagged by the rung that may ask it. Disciplines pick from it; no discipline runs it whole.

# Question bank

Each question carries the origin of the idea behind it, for the maintainer.

## sharpen · round one (always settled, exactly these three)
- What do you want out of building this: to learn something, to ship something, to make money, or to have it in your portfolio? *(judging criterion)*
- How many weeks are you willing to give the first version? *(appetite; Shape Up)*
- Who is the one person you can picture using this, and when was the last time they hit the problem? Tell it as it happened. *(struggling moment; Moesta, Mom Test)*

## sharpen · job story
- What was going on right before that moment that made "good enough" stop being good enough? *(push; Moesta)*
- What did they want to be able to do afterwards? *(outcome; Klement)*
- Is this among that person's top three problems, or a nice-to-have? *(painkiller vs vitamin; Fitzpatrick)*
- Do you have this problem yourself? If not, how do you know it exists? *(Graham, Levels)*

## sharpen · status quo
- What do they use today, including nothing, and what do they call it? *(competitive alternative; Dunford, Christensen)*
- What have they already tried, and why did it fail? Why have they not solved it already? *(Fitzpatrick)*
- Have they spent time, money, or reputation on this before? *(currency; Fitzpatrick)*
- What would they have to stop using or unlearn to adopt this? *(habit; Moesta)*

## sharpen · four forces
- What would they imagine life being like with this? *(pull; Moesta)*
- What would worry them about switching: data, time, looking foolish, it not working? *(anxiety; Moesta)*
- Which force is weakest, and does push + pull beat anxiety + habit today? *(verdict)*

## sharpen · well or puddle
- Is this something few people want a lot, or many people want a little? *(Graham)*
- Who wants this right now, in a rough first version? Name them, or name where they gather. *(Graham)*
- Is there a path from that group to a larger one? *(concentric circles; Thiel)*

## spar · choosing
- Which alternative, or merge, and what does it give up that you accept? *(trade-off)*
- What is the hero feature of the direction you chose, in one sentence a demo could show? *(epicenter)*
- For each rejected alternative: why did it lose, in one line? *(ledger)*

## spar · pre-mortem
- It is a year from now and this failed completely. What are the reasons, especially the ones nobody would say out loud? *(Klein)*
- Which reason is most likely, and which is easiest to prevent now? *(Klein)*
- Who would certainly never use this, and why? *(inversion)*

## shape · problem story and epicenter
- What is the one story that shows the status quo failing, for a reader who has never met this person? *(Shape Up pitch)*
- If this feature were removed, would the product still make sense? *(epicenter test; Getting Real)*
- What is the whole usable path through the job that the first version delivers, end to end? *(skateboard; Kniberg)*

## shape · Kano sort
- If this feature were present, how would the person feel? If absent? *(Kano functional and dysfunctional)*
- Which features would they never praise but would notice missing? *(must-be)*
- Which one feature is the reason to switch? *(delighter)*

## shape · appetite fit
- Is this possible in the appetite? *(Shape Up)*
- Where does the build depend on something nobody has verified works? *(rabbit hole)*
- What is explicitly out of bounds for this version? *(no-go)*
- Walking the use case slowly, where would a builder get stuck for a week? *(rabbit hole)*

## shape · riskiest assumption
- What has to be true for this to work? Decompose until each assumption is a single claim. *(Higham)*
- Which of those is most important and has the least evidence behind it? *(Bland)*
- What is the smallest, cheapest thing that could prove it wrong, and what number means it passed? *(Higham, Torres)*
- If the test involves talking to people, what past behaviour or commitment would count as evidence? *(Fitzpatrick)*

## shape · second act
- When the hero feature is copied, what is left? A 10x dimension, a secret, or nothing yet? *(Kano decay; Thiel)*
- Would they use it once or twice a day? If not, what is its natural frequency? *(toothbrush test; Page)*

## position · alternatives and value
- What would the target person do today if this product did not exist, in their words? *(Dunford)*
- Is "build it myself" or "do nothing" one of the alternatives, and how is it addressed? *(Markepear, Dunford)*
- For each unique attribute: so what, for that person? Repeat until it terminates in value they feel. *(Dunford)*
- What proof exists for that value from someone like the target? *(Dunford; Moore's reference base)*

## position · category and statement
- What existing category, or sub-segment of one, makes the value obvious? Which categories were evaluated and rejected? *(Dunford)*
- What trend, if any, makes this relevant now, and is the connection obvious without explanation? *(Dunford)*
- For [target] who [need], [product] is a [category] that [benefit]. Unlike [alternative], [differentiator]. Does it survive being said aloud in an elevator? *(Moore)*

## position · audiences and channels
- How many people in your own network match the job story? Name the count. *(trust circle; Rachitsky)*
- Where does the target person already gather, by name (a subreddit, a Discord, a list, a registry, a meetup), and what comparable product landed there? *(Rachitsky; Traction)*
- What makes the second audience adjacent to the first: a shared person or a shared use? *(bowling alley; Moore)*
- Which single channel is the primary bet, which two are the cheap tests, and what does each cost in time and money? *(Bullseye; Weinberg and Mares)*
- Does the price and lifetime value support any paid channel at this stage? *(Walling)*
- Is the product inside an ecosystem that supplies customers (a marketplace, a plugin directory, a registry), and what does listing there require? *(Walling)*

## position · evidence gate and launch series
- How many users will be asked "how would you feel if you could no longer use this", and what share saying "very disappointed" gates scaled marketing? *(Ellis; Vohra)*
- What are the first three launches, each aimed at which audience, and what is different in each? *(Linear; Rachitsky)*
- Does each launch comply with the venue's rules? *(venues.md)*
- Which artifacts do the chosen channels require, as opposed to generic collateral? *(Show HN norms; docs as marketing)*
