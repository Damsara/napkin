# Venues

A launch that breaks a venue's rules is a failed launch. Check each planned launch against its row before writing it into the spec.

| Venue | Rule that gets launches removed | Current state (2026-08) | What works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Show HN | Must be something people can run or try; no landing pages, sign-up walls, waitlists, newsletters, or version bumps. Never ask anyone to upvote or comment. Reposts allowed after about a year without traction. | Volume tripled in 2025 to about 200 Show HNs a day. Median score 2 points; 50+ points is the top 6%. Each upvote is worth roughly 1.4 GitHub stars in 48 hours, and star gain is over within 48 hours. Monday 00:00 UTC has the best odds. | Link the repo, not a landing page. Explain how and why it was built in the first comment. Substance over polish. |
| Product Hunt | Featuring is curated: useful, novel, high craft; waitlists, directories, templates, and monetisation-first minimal products are excluded. | About 10% of launches are featured; unfeatured launches get a fraction of the traffic. Reported yields for indie launches: hundreds of visits, tens of signups. | Credibility and feedback more than customers. Needs an audience brought along; a landing page belongs here, not on HN. |
| Reddit | Be a redditor with a product, not a product with a Reddit account. Participate for weeks before promoting. Many subreddits restrict self-promotion to designated threads. | r/SideProject and r/SaaS welcome founder stories with context; r/startups only in designated threads; r/Entrepreneur restricted. Link drops are removed. | A story post without a link, answering questions in the comments. |
| X, build in public | No platform rule; reach is the constraint. Links in the post body cut reach sharply. | The most-used channel among bootstrapped apps; the audience is mostly other makers, not customers. Failure posts outperform win posts. Every app past meaningful revenue also ran another channel. | A warm audience for the other launches. Never the only channel. |
| Newsletters (sponsored) | Paid placement; price scales with list size. | Niche developer lists from a few hundred dollars per issue; large ones in the thousands. | Only after the evidence gate, and only when lifetime value supports it. |
| Registries and directories (npm, skills.sh, marketplaces, plugin stores) | Listing rules vary; the artifact is the README and the install count. | skills.sh discovers by search and install count; Claude Code's official marketplace accepts plugins by review. | A product inside an ecosystem gets customers from the ecosystem; the README is the landing page. |
| GitHub Trending | Undocumented; relative star velocity within a language and window. | A front-page HN post is the usual driver; the effect decays within a week. | A consequence of another launch, not a launch. |
| Personal network | None. | The most common source of first customers in every dataset consulted. | Named people who match the job story, asked one at a time. |
