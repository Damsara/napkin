# Position

Call the Skill tool with "canon".

If the ledger has no `shaped` stage, ask for the product in one paragraph, its hero feature, and the person it is for, and write them to decisions. Where the Marketer seat's minutes exist, rungs 1 to 4 are confirmations of what the board already recorded, asked as one round.

Rounds per canon. Questions come from the `position` sections of canon's `references/questions.md`. The output is strategy: a statement, audiences, bets, a gate, launch names, artifact names.

## Ladder
1. **Competitive alternatives.** What the target person does today if the product does not exist, in their words, from the ledger and `landscape.md`. Build-it-myself and do-nothing are candidates.
2. **Attributes to value.** The hero feature's unique attributes; "so what" on each until it terminates in value the person feels; proof, or "unproven".
3. **Category frame.** An existing category, or a sub-segment of one, that makes the value obvious. Choose the sub-segment unless the builder supplies an education budget and a horizon in years. Write every category evaluated to the ledger's rejected list with why it lost.
4. **Statement.** *For [target] who [need], [product] is a [category] that [benefit]. Unlike [alternative], [differentiator].* Cut until the two sentences total at most forty words.
5. **Audiences by trust circle.** First, named people in the builder's network who match the job story, with the count. Second, the venues from `landscape.md` where the person gathers, each with a precedent URL. Third, press or a directory launch. Three audiences, ordered, each with what makes it adjacent to the last.
6. **Channel bets.** At most three tests, each with its cost in time and money, how many of the right people it reaches, and how the builder will know it worked. One is the primary bet. Paid channels only where price and lifetime value support them.
7. **Evidence gate.** Before any paid or scaled channel: about forty users asked "how would you feel if you could no longer use this", and 40% answering "very disappointed". Write it as a gate in the spec.
8. **Launch series.** Three launches, each aimed at one audience, each different from the last, each checked row by row against canon's `references/venues.md`. A launch that breaks a venue rule is rewritten or dropped.
9. **Artifacts.** What the chosen channels require (a runnable demo, a README, docs, a comparison page, a founder story, an invite mechanism), named per launch.

## Exit
Write section 7 of the spec per canon's `references/spec-format.md`, write `stage: positioned` to the ledger, and tell the user the spec's path. Every audience has an order and an adjacency; every channel has a cost; every launch has a venue check.
